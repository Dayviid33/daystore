Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DayStore collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

DayStore does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the site. We do not collect names, email addresses, or payment details directly.

We use your browser's local storage to remember your saved favorites and your light/dark theme preference. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or any third party — it's simply used to make the site work the way you left it on your next visit.

Affiliate Links & Third-Party Sites

DayStore contains affiliate links to third-party retailers, such as Amazon. When you click one of these links, the retailer may place a cookie on your browser to track that the visit came from DayStore, so we can earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Once you leave our site, that retailer's own privacy policy governs how your information is handled.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics tools in the future to understand which pages and products are popular. If we do, this policy will be updated to reflect exactly what's collected.

Cookies

Aside from cookies set by third-party retailers through affiliate links, DayStore itself does not currently set tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage (used for favorites and theme preference) is not a cookie and is never transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

DayStore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, feel free to reach out through our contact page.
